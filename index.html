How do I check availability?

Check the availability highlights on Isha’s Instagram, then send your date and cake idea. Your date is confirmed directly with Isha.

Can you work from my inspiration?

Share the colours, theme and details you love with your enquiry. Isha can discuss a bespoke design suited to your celebration.

What about allergies and dietary needs?

Please mention any allergies or dietary requirements when you enquire. Ask Isha to confirm ingredients and suitability before booking.
