# Bakes by Isha

Responsive landing-page preview built from the selected design mockups. Buildless HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on Vercel.

## Design

Cream and blush surfaces, chocolate outlines, burgundy accents, original Instagram logo and original cake photography. Allura is a visual approximation for the handwritten headings; the authentic logo is preserved as an image.

Mobile: scalloped header, overlapping framed hero slideshow, text-only about section, 1.66 square category cards per mobile view, cake enquiry ticket and persistent bottom action. Desktop: balanced two-column hero, four collection cards and three-column process section.

Motion: staged hero entrances, six-second crossfades, swipe and dot controls, pause button, downward menu drawer, reveal-on-scroll, and reduced-motion support.

## Enquiries

The three-step modal drawer validates occasion, design and name, preserves entered answers during the session, and prepares a personalised message for visitors to copy and send to @bakes.byisha on Instagram. It does not send email, collect uploads, store enquiry data or confirm bookings. No private contact details, fake reviews or invented prices are included. The kind-words carousel quotes three verified public Instagram comments, attributed and linked to their original comments; these are community reactions, not verified purchase testimonials.
